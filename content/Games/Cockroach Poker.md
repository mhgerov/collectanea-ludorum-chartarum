## Equipment
Tarot deck with trump suit 16-21 removed
## Objective
Palm off as much of your cards as possible. Player who runs out of cards in their hand or collects 7 of a given suit is the loser.
## Preparation
Set aside Fool (0) and The Devil (15). Shuffle the deck, then place 16 cards face down into a pile. Reshuffle the Fool and Devil into the main pile, then deal equally to all the players.
## Play
Youngest player starts
### Attempt to Pass
The player takes a card from their hand and passes it face-down to another, claiming it's one of the suits or *Royal* to indicate a court card. The receiving player can choose to say that the statement is true/false or look at it and try to pass to another player.

If the receiving player is correct, then the player that attempted to pass has to place it face-up in front of them. If not, then the receiving player places it face-up
### Royals
If the card is a royal, then another card has to be drawn from the royal pile and placed face-up in addition to the first card. It may daisy-chain if the drawn card is a royal too.
### Special Cards
**The Devil (VX)**: Any claim is true, except for "Royal"
**The Fool**: Any claim is false

Loser adds the special card to their hand, and lays the claimed type from their hand face-up. If the player is unable or unwilling to do so, then they must place two cards down of other types.
